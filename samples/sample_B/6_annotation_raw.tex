\noindent (i) Let $X$ is a metric space, with $\mathcal{A}$ -- open cover of $X$, then if $X$ is compact, then $\exists \; \delta > 0$ s.t. $\forall \; S \subseteq$ subset $S$ of $X$ with $\operatorname{diam}(S) < \delta$, $S$ is contained in some $A \in \mathcal{A}$.
